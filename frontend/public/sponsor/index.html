Why now

Chrome removes built-in XSLT in version 158, on 17 November 2026. Every site that renders XML with a stylesheet in the browser has to move that transformation somewhere else, and the people doing that work are searching for answers. Our guide to the change already brings in around 600 visits a month from Google, with the deadline still ahead.
